# SilverScreens New Landing Page Design — Preview & Features

## 🎬 Design Overview

The new landing page has been completely redesigned with a **maximum cinematic luxury aesthetic** that rivals Netflix, Apple TV+, Vogue, and IMDb Pro.

**Live Preview:** http://localhost:3000/preview-landing

## ✨ Key Features & Sections

### 1. **Hero Section (100vh)**
- **Ultra-cinematic** dark gradient background with gold radial glows
- **Massive oversized headline** using Bebas Neue: "DISCOVER. CONNECT. GET CAST."
- Animated eyebrow with gold lines and premium copy
- Large, readable subheadline with italic styling
- **Dual CTAs** with premium styling:
  - Gold button: "Join As Talent" (luxury hover effects)
  - Border button: "Hire Talent" (gold glow on hover)
- **Animated scroll indicator** with bouncing animation
- **Stats bar** at bottom with 4 KPIs (100K+ Talents, 20K+ Calls, 5K+ Stories, 500+ Agencies)
- Custom animated scan lines for cinematic effect
- Glassmorphism navigation that appears on scroll

### 2. **Featured Talents Section**
- **Glassmorphism card design** with backdrop blur
- 4 talent cards in grid layout
- Cards feature:
  - Large avatar placeholder with gold gradient
  - Name, category, and location
  - "Featured" badge for standout talents
  - Hover overlay with premium "View Profile" button
  - Smooth transition effects
- Gold borders and accent lines

### 3. **How It Works Section**
- **5-step horizontal timeline** with animated circles
- Each step shows:
  - Numbered circle (01-05) with gold styling
  - Title and description
  - Horizontal connecting line (animated)
- Premium hover effects that elevate the circle and change text to gold
- Elegant spacing and typography

### 4. **Trending Casting Calls Section**
- 3 casting opportunity cards
- Each card includes:
  - Project title
  - Roles needed
  - Location with pin emoji
  - Budget range
  - Deadline
  - Premium "Apply Now →" button
- Glassmorphism background
- Animated top accent line on hover

### 5. **Success Stories Section**
- 3 editorial-style quote cards
- Features:
  - Large decorative quote mark (subtle, low opacity)
  - Client testimonial in light italic
  - Author name and role
  - Premium gradient background
  - Smooth hover transitions

### 6. **CTA Banner Section**
- **Full-width cinematic banner** with gradient background
- Large headline: "YOUR NEXT OPPORTUNITY / IS WAITING"
- Subheadline with social proof
- Dual CTAs matching hero section
- Radial gold glow in background

### 7. **Footer**
- Premium dark footer with gold accents
- 4-column layout:
  - Brand section with logo and description
  - Navigate links (About, Talents, Casting, Pricing)
  - Legal links (Terms, Privacy, Contact, Support)
  - Social media icons (I, T, L, Y for Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube)
- Bottom copyright and tagline

## 🎨 Visual Style & Branding

### Colors
- **Background:** `#050505` (pure black)
- **Surfaces:** `#0B0F14`, `#121821` (dark charcoal)
- **Primary Gold:** `#D4A64A` (warm, luxurious)
- **Text:** `#F5F5F5` (off-white cream)
- **Secondary Text:** `#A8B0BD` (muted silver)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Bebas Neue (oversized, luxury cinema feel)
- **Body:** Montserrat (clean, modern, professional)
- **Font Weights:** 300 (light), 400 (regular), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold), 900 (ultra)

### Effects & Animations
- ✨ **Custom cursor** (gold dot with expanding ring on hover)
- 🎬 **Cinematic gradients** (radial gold glows, linear fades)
- ✨ **Glassmorphism** throughout (backdrop-blur, semi-transparent backgrounds)
- 🌊 **Smooth scroll reveals** (elements fade in and slide up)
- ⚡ **Premium hover effects** (scale, color transitions, glows)
- 📊 **Animated stats** (counters, interactive hovers)
- 📜 **Scan line animations** (subtle, adds cinematic quality)
